NASA C-MAPSS FD001 - Data provenance and published-use information

This file records the source and published licensing status of the dataset
redistributed in this repository. It is not an official NASA license text,
not a standardized open-data license, and not a new grant of rights.

1. DATA COVERED

The underlying data is the FD001 subset of the Turbofan Engine Degradation
Simulation Data Set provided by the NASA Ames Prognostics Center of
Excellence. This repository includes CSV exports, a reformatted Excel
workbook, and videos and images generated from those simulated measurements.
Repository-specific transformations are described in NOTICE.md and the
data/README.md and video/README.md files.

2. ORIGINAL SOURCES

NASA dataset catalog:
https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/cmapss-jet-engine-simulated-data

NASA Prognostics Center of Excellence Data Set Repository:
https://www.nasa.gov/intelligent-systems-division/discovery-and-systems-health/pcoe/pcoe-data-set-repository/

4. ATTRIBUTION INFORMATION

NASA's repository requests acknowledgment of the repository and data
contributors in publications that use its datasets. The dataset citation is:

A. Saxena and K. Goebel (2008). Turbofan Engine Degradation Simulation
Data Set. NASA Prognostics Data Repository, NASA Ames Research Center,
Moffett Field, CA.

The original data-generation reference is:

A. Saxena, K. Goebel, D. Simon, and N. Eklund (2008). Damage Propagation
Modeling for Aircraft Engine Run-to-Failure Simulation. Proceedings of the
International Conference on Prognostics and Health Management (PHM08),
Denver, CO.
